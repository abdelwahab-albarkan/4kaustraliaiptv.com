Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the calculator?

It gives a planning estimate using typical speeds per stream (SD 3, HD 5, Full HD 8, 4K 25 Mbps) plus the headroom you choose. Real streams vary, so treat it as a guide.

Why does it add headroom?

Other devices in your home also use the internet, and a stream needs spare capacity to stay smooth at peak times.

Which NBN plan should I pick?

Choose the plan whose typical evening speed is above the number the calculator shows. Check the typical evening speed your provider publishes.
