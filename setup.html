The Australian NBN DNS Anti-Buffering Guide

Major Australian internet providers (including Telstra, Optus, TPG, and Vodafone) occasionally apply aggressive transparent DNS caches during peak prime-time hours (Friday to Sunday evening footy). Setting your DNS to Cloudflare or Google Anycast bypasses this limitation, delivering instant channel switching and smooth streaming:

Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 (Recommended for Sydney/Melbourne)

Primary DNS: 1.1.1.1 | Secondary DNS: 1.0.0.1

Sub-5ms response time on all Australian NBN nodes.

Google Secure DNS (Recommended for Regional AU)

Primary DNS: 8.8.8.8 | Secondary DNS: 8.8.4.4

Maximum compatibility across Telstra 5G & Starlink AU.
