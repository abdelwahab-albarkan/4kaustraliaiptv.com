IPTV player questions

What is the best IPTV player in Australia?

TiviMate is a favourite on Fire TV Stick and Android TV boxes. IPTV Smarters Pro is the most compatible across phones, tablets and PCs. IBO Player is common on Samsung and LG smart TVs.

Does an IPTV player include channels?

No. An IPTV player is only the app. You add the login details or playlist from your IPTV provider to see channels.

What is the difference between M3U and Xtream Codes?

An M3U playlist is a single link to your channel list. Xtream Codes uses a server URL, username and password and also loads the programme guide and movies more neatly. Most players support both.

Why are my channels buffering in my IPTV player?

Buffering is often your network. Try a wired connection, restart the router, or change DNS. If a single channel fails, try another. Read our IPTV buffering guide for a step-by-step fix.

Can I use the free trial with any IPTV player?

Yes. Start a free trial and enter the details in TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, IBO Player or another supported player.
