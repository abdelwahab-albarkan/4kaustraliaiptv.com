About 4K Australia IPTV

4K Australia IPTV is an IPTV information and subscription service for viewers in Australia. We publish setup guides, device and player comparisons, and offer a free 24-hour trial so you can test the service on your own TV and internet before you pay.

What we do

  • IPTV subscriptions in Australian dollars, with 1, 3, 6 and 12-month plans.
  • A free 24-hour trial with no credit card, so you can test live channels on your own connection.
  • Setup guides for Fire TV Stick, Android TV boxes, Apple TV, Samsung and LG TVs, phones and PCs.
  • A reseller panel for people who want to offer IPTV to their own customers.

How we work

We aim to explain things plainly: what an IPTV box or player does, how to test a service, and what to check before you pay. Our guides are written for people watching in Australia on NBN and mobile connections.
