IPTV EPG Explained: Fix an Empty or Wrong TV Guide

The EPG is your IPTV TV guide. Here is how it works and how to fix it when it is empty, missing channels or showing the wrong times.

The programme guide, or EPG, turns a long list of channels into a TV guide you can browse. When it works you barely notice it. When it is empty or out by an hour it is one of the most common IPTV complaints, and it is usually easy to fix.

How the guide gets into your player

Your IPTV player loads two things: the channel list and the guide data. Some services include the guide automatically with an Xtream Codes login. Others give you a separate XMLTV link to paste into your player’s EPG settings.

Fix an empty or missing guide

  1. 1

    Refresh the playlist and guide

    In your player, find the refresh or update option for the playlist and EPG, then wait a few minutes for it to finish.

  2. 2

    Check the EPG source

    Make sure an EPG or XMLTV link is set if your service gives you one. Paste it exactly with no spaces.

  3. 3

    Match channels to the guide

    Some players let you map a channel to its guide listing. Do this for channels that still show no information.

  4. 4

    Restart the app

    Close the player fully and reopen it. On a TV box, restart the box.

Fix guide times that are wrong

Set the time zone in your device and in the player’s settings. Many players also have a manual EPG time shift option to adjust the guide by one or more hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EPG in IPTV?

EPG stands for Electronic Programme Guide. It is the on-screen TV guide that shows what is on now and next for each channel.

Why is my IPTV guide empty?

The most common causes are a guide that has not finished loading, an EPG source that is not linked in your player, or a wrong time zone. Refresh the playlist and the guide, then check your player’s EPG settings.

Why are the guide times wrong?

The guide and your device must use the same time zone. Australia has several (AEST/AEDT, ACST/ACDT and AWST) and daylight saving changes the offset for some states.

What is XMLTV?

XMLTV is a common file format for TV guide data. Many IPTV players can load an XMLTV link to fill the guide.
