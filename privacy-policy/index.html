Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information 4K Australia IPTV collects when you use 4kaustraliaiptv.com, request a free trial, buy a plan or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, device type and the messages you send us.
  • Order information: the plan you choose and the payment reference you send us. We do not store full card numbers on this website.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through analytics if enabled.

How we use it

  • To create your free trial or subscription and send your login details.
  • To answer support questions by email or WhatsApp.
  • To improve our guides and website, and to understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations and prevent misuse of our service.

Cookies and analytics

We save a small amount of data in your browser to remember your choices, such as the light or dark theme and your cookie choice. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are only set if you press Accept in the cookie banner, and they are not loaded if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer, or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the cdnjs content network. When you click a WhatsApp link, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. Payment providers handle your payment details under their own policies.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the service (for example email, messaging and payment providers), or when the law requires it.

Keeping your information

We keep your information only as long as needed for your subscription, support and legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop receiving messages from us. Email support@4kaustraliaiptv.com or message us on WhatsApp at +212 625 218 443.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
