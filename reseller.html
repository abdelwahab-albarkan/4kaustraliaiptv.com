Before you start an IPTV reselling business

Reselling IPTV can be a side business, but it comes with responsibilities. Think through these points before you buy credits.

  • Licensing: make sure the supply you resell has the rights to its content. You are responsible for what you sell. See the legality guide.
  • Support time: customers will message you about setup and buffering. Our device guides can help.
  • Pricing: work out your margin with the profit calculator.
  • Terms and refunds: publish clear terms and a refund policy, like ours: Refund Policy.
  • Trials: a short trial helps customers decide and reduces refund requests.
