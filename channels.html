How to check a channel line-up before you subscribe

A long channel number does not tell you whether your channels are there. Check the line-up for the channels you actually watch.

  1. 1

    List your must-haves

    Free-to-air, news, kids and the sport you follow.

  2. 2

    Ask for the channel list

    A service should be able to tell you what is included.

  3. 3

    Test during a live event

    Watch a match or news bulletin, not just a movie.

  4. 4

    Check the guide

    Open the programme guide and confirm times match your time zone.
