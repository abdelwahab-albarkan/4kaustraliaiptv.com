How to compare IPTV providers in Australia

Provider lists are only a starting point. What matters is how a service behaves on your own internet at the times you watch. Use these checks to compare any two providers fairly.

  • Rights and licensing: does the service say where its channels come from? Read our legality guide.
  • A free trial: you should be able to test before paying.
  • Stability on your NBN: test during live sport on a Friday or Saturday night.
  • Channels and sport: check your must-have channels are listed.
  • Devices and apps: confirm it works with your IPTV player and box.
  • Clear pricing and support: prices in AUD and a way to reach a person.

A fair test in four steps

  1. 1

    Use the same device

    Test every provider on the same TV or box.

  2. 2

    Use the same connection

    Prefer a cable to rule out Wi-Fi.

  3. 3

    Test the same match

    Watch the same live event on each.

  4. 4

    Write down what you saw

    Note start time, picture, freezes and how fast support replied.
